Division Super Admin
Schools Division Office (SDO Level)
Super Admin
Primary Persona:Division Superintendent / EPS / SDO IT
Jurisdiction:Division-Wide (All 25 High Schools)
Selector Mode:Unlocked ("ALL" or Campus Switch)
Exclusive Division Privileges
  • School Matrix Management: Exclusive access to tab-school-matrix to add, configure, or retire secondary schools in Valenzuela City.
  • SDO Invite Code Issuance: Generates secure one-time authorization tokens (SDO-XXXX-YYYY) to authorize School Principals.
  • Direct Administrator Provisioning: Ability to directly provision and bind campus administrator credentials without invite codes.
  • Division Spotlight Benchmark: Monitors institutional literacy trends, Phil-IRI reading distributions, and assessment heatmaps.
  • Permanent Account Deletion: Sole governance tier authorized to permanently remove faculty/admin records from Firebase.
Campus School Admin
Individual Campus (School Principal)
School Admin
Primary Persona:School Principal / Head Teacher / School ICT
Jurisdiction:Single DepEd School ID (e.g. 305441)
Selector Mode:Permanently Locked to Assigned School
Exclusive Campus Responsibilities
  • Faculty Accreditation Authority: Evaluates newly registered teachers and grants 1-click approval to enable mobile app classroom access.
  • Classroom Section Governance: Oversees campus sections, advisory assignments, and student roster enrollments.
  • Student Credential Vault: Batch-generates student accounts, manages onboarding tokens, and exports printable student access slips.
  • Pedagogical Oversight & Remarks: Conducts read-only inspection of reading modules, exercises, and diagnostic assessments with the Remarks drawer.
  • Protected Campus Scoping: System strictly hides the School Matrix and blocks access to records outside their assigned DepEd School ID.

DepEd Campus Onboarding & Accreditation Lifecycle Institutional Security Flow
Step 01 • SDO
Issue SDO Invite
Super Admin generates a single-use token (SDO-XXXX-YYYY) in the School Matrix for the principal.
Step 02 • Campus
Claim Workspace
School Admin claims the invite code on the Web Portal, binding their account irreversibly to their School ID.
Step 03 • Teacher
Mobile Registration
Educators sign up via the Android app and are automatically flagged as Pending Review.
Step 04 • Approval
Accredit Faculty
Campus School Admin clicks Approve in the Faculty Registry, unlocking mobile teaching tools.
