Built for students and teachers in the classroom

Both students and teachers use the native GabAI Android app. Teachers run their classrooms directly on mobile without needing complex software, while students read with confidence.

For Students

Never feel stuck or embarrassed by difficult vocabulary. The floating bubble stays with you on screen while you read PDF modules, browser articles, or study materials.

  • Tap any word to hear natural audio pronunciation instantly
  • Simplified definitions with contextual examples to understand unfamiliar words
  • Bite-sized comprehension mini-quizzes to lock in retention

For Teachers

Manage your classes completely within the GabAI mobile app. Enroll your entire class in seconds, assign reading passages, and track vocabulary mastery without paperwork.

  • Instant classroom QR codes via Google Code Scanner
  • In-app dashboard shows which words challenge your students
  • Direct mobile workflow designed for classroom teachers

How to get started in three simple steps

Both teachers and students get up and running entirely on mobile.

1

Install the Mobile App

Download and install the GabAI Android App on any phone or tablet running Android 8.0 or newer.

2

Scan Your Classroom QR

Students scan the classroom QR code generated on the teacher's mobile screen to join without typing usernames or passwords.

3

Tap the Floating Bubble

Open your reading module. Tap the floating assistant whenever you need word definitions, pronunciations, or review quizzes.

Frequently asked questions

Everything students, teachers, and school administrators need to know.

Do teachers use this website or the mobile app?

Teachers use the mobile app exclusively! All class creation, QR code scanning, assignment workflows, and student progress tracking happen directly inside the GabAI Android app.

Who is authorized to access the Admin Portal?

The web Admin Portal is strictly reserved for authorized School Administrators and coordinators for institutional setup and campus records.

What Android versions are supported?

GabAI runs on devices running Android 8.0 (Oreo) and above. It is engineered to perform smoothly on standard mobile devices used in classrooms.

Does the app require an internet connection?

Yes, GabAI requires an active internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data) to power real-time AI vocabulary explanations, generated quizzes, and classroom synchronization.
