Frequently Asked Questions & Governance Rules

Why can't Super Admins or School Admins use the mobile app?

By GabAI platform architecture, administrative governance (school records, teacher verification, and school analytics) is managed via this desktop Web Portal. The Android mobile app is strictly reserved for Teachers (classroom instruction) and Students (reading assistant, gamified quests, and word scanner).

How do SDO Invite Codes work?

The Schools Division Office (SDO) issues a single confidential invite code (SDO-XXXX-YYYY) per secondary school. The School Principal or designated IT Coordinator uses this code to claim and activate their school's administrative workspace. Each code is one-time use and irreversibly binds the admin account to their DepEd School ID.

How do Teacher Approvals work?

When an educator signs up via the mobile app, their account is flagged as Pending Review. In accordance with DepEd school jurisdiction governance, they cannot log into the mobile app until their assigned School Administrator clicks Approve in the Faculty Registry tab. The Division Super Admin (SDO) monitors division-wide accreditation status for institutional audit.

How do Administrative Remarks work?

Admins have read-only oversight over instructional materials. To provide instructional notes, curriculum alignments, or academic intervention recommendations, click the Remarks button next to any PDF material or weekly assessment. Your remarks are logged with your official administrative designation.
